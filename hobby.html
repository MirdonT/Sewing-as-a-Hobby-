Sew with me as a beginner

The reason why I am taking a sewing class this summer term of my sophomore year is because I love thrifting. Most common problems I encounter when thrifting is not having the right size or length for my height, so learning how to make alterations will be a great skill to learn!

  1. The supplies you will be needing for your first day sewing:
    • 18x3 Ruler
    • Shears
    • Tailor's Chalk
    • Measuring Tape
    • Seam Ripper
    • Pins
    • Pin Cushion
    • Sewing Gauge
    • Choice of Fabrics
    • Snips
  2. Extra supplies you will be needing in the future
    • Needles for hand sewing and machine needles
    • Iron and Iron board
    • Taylor's Ham
    • Sewing clips
    • Variety of threads
    • Pins
    • Extra bobbins
    • Buttons
    • Tracing Papers
    • Rotary Cutter
